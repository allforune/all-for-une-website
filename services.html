Services

We lease it. We run it. Nothing to pay.

We take your property on as your corporate tenant — not as a letting agent — and think about three things at once: the property, the owner, and the people staying there.

The terms
A tenant you can rely on, and nothing to pay for it.
01
Guaranteed rent

We lease the property as your corporate tenant and pay the agreed rent on the agreed date, every month — whether it's occupied or not.

02
No void periods

Finding and keeping occupants is our job. Your rent does not pause between them.

03
No fees to you

No management, set-up or letting fees. Our work is paid for by how we run the property, not by you.

Every term is agreed and set out in writing before anything begins.

The standard

Nine things we hold ourselves to on every property.

Not a checklist we mention once. A standard we're assessed against every time we inspect, and every time you ask.

  • Property oversight
    One named person who knows the building, its quirks and its neighbours.
  • Presentation
    Every room checked against the same written standard before anyone arrives.
  • Cleaning
    Professional, every changeover. Linen inspected, not assumed.
  • Maintenance
    Small faults fixed while they are small, by trades we know and trust.
  • Furnishing
    Durable, considered pieces chosen to last, not just to photograph well.
  • Inspections
    Regular walk-throughs with notes and photographs, shared with you.
  • Issue management
    Every problem logged, owned and closed. You hear about it from us first.
  • Communication
    A plain-English update on a set rhythm, and a person to call in between.
  • Occupancy
    House rules that protect the neighbours and the building, kindly enforced.

Property ownership, without the parts you never wanted to do yourself.
