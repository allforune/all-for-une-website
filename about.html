Our story

We were landlords first.

When we moved abroad, we handed our London home to a management company. It was let, but it wasn't looked after. Problems arrived late. The work, when it came, was done badly.

We kept thinking the same thing: we could have done this better.

We already knew what better looked like.

We have always loved doing up a home — rented or owned. In the US, we redid our place from top to bottom. It gave us a home we loved, and when we sold it, the gain was 69% on what we paid for it.

Care and attention to a property don't cost money. They earn it back.

So we built the service we wished we'd had.

We're Abhishek and Ayushi, and we do everything together. Ayushi leads on design. Abhishek runs the numbers. All for Une manages London homes for landlords with the property, but not the time or patience, to improve it themselves.

We lease your home directly, as your corporate tenant. You get guaranteed rent, on the agreed date, every month — occupied or not. No void periods. No management, set-up or letting fees. Our work is paid for by how we run the property, not by you.

You get one named contact, a dated record of every check and repair, and a straight answer on your options. If you'd like the home refreshed before it's let, we'll show you what it adds to the rent and how quickly it lets. If selling is better than letting, we'll say so.
