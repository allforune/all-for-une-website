How we work
02

Being a landlord has become complicated.

The rules keep changing. The responsibilities keep growing. Maintenance, compliance and the day-to-day of whoever is living there all land on you, and costs have not been kind. Many good landlords are quietly asking whether it is still worth the effort.

  • Changing regulation
  • Growing responsibilities
  • Maintenance that never quite ends
  • Compliance to keep on top of
  • Managing who lives there
  • Rising costs

That doesn't necessarily mean selling. It might mean having a better partner.

03

We look after the property. Not just the tenancy.

We take your property on as your corporate tenant, and we think about three things at once. Most operators only think about one.

The property

Presented, maintained, protected.

Kept to a high standard, looked at regularly and fixed early, so it is worth as much when we hand it back as when we took it on.

The owner

Clear, dependable, commercial.

Rent when it is due. Updates in plain English. Decisions made with the numbers in mind, and nothing left for you to chase.

The people staying there

Thoughtful hospitality.

We want the people staying in your home to enjoy it. Guests who are looked after tend to look after the place in return.

What this means for you
A tenant you can rely on, and nothing to pay for it.
Guaranteed rent

We lease the property as your corporate tenant and pay the agreed rent on the agreed date, every month.

No void periods

Finding and keeping occupants is our job. Your rent does not pause between them.

No fees to you

No management, set-up or letting fees. Our work is paid for by how we run the property, not by you.

Every term is agreed and set out in writing before anything begins.

We're quite particular about property.

Our standards go beyond the basics. They're about protecting your asset, creating a better experience for the people staying there and making sure you're always informed.

Our approach →
  • Property oversight
    One named person who knows the building, its quirks and its neighbours.
  • Presentation

Questions landlords ask.

How does guaranteed rent work?+

We lease your property as your corporate tenant and pay the agreed rent on the agreed date each month, whether or not it is occupied. Terms are set out in writing before anything begins.

Does it cost me anything?+

No. There are no management, set-up or letting fees to you.

Who looks after repairs and maintenance?+

We do. We stay close to the property, deal with issues proactively and keep you informed without burdening you with the detail.

Will the property be returned in good condition?+

That is the standard we work to. We record the condition at the start, inspect regularly and share what we find.

What kinds of property do you take on?+

Well-located London homes we can look after to a high standard. We assess each one individually and tell you honestly if we are not the right fit.

I'm thinking of selling. Should I still talk to you?+

Yes. You do not need to decide first. We will understand what you are trying to achieve and give you an honest view.
